# Operational guardrails

To stop the cut from pretending to be the room. Every claim passes
these five or it is not a claim yet.

```
Is the sentence explicit about V, G, θ?
        NO  →  REJECT: not a claim yet
        YES →
              Do two presentations agree on this V?
                    YES → grow V before calling them one G
                    NO  → treat as distinct cuts
```

1. **Explicit triple.** A sentence that cannot name `V`, `G`, and `θ`
   is unparsed. Not a process claim.
2. **Equivalence domain.** Two presentations that match on a small `V`
   are not identical. Grow `V` and look for a split first.
3. **Boundary.** A step that leaves `V` does not append a point.
   Fire `θ` or run a registered `μ`.
4. **No free renaming.** A label that changes no knob is a comment.
   It does not accrue insight and it does not accrue cost.
5. **Unified ledger.** Work that happened is a line. Zero-cost work
   is an accounting failure.

Nouns compress executable cuts. The noun does not get to run.
Replica, receipt, ledger line — or it did not happen.

`G` is the declared mechanism of *this* presentation, not a pure
mechanism of the world. “Executable graph” is a noun for the cut,
not a new inhabitant.
